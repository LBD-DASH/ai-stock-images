# Adobe Stock Contributor setup checklist (South African individual)

One-page checklist for Kevin. Drafts only. No account was created in this run. All links are official Adobe help pages (checked 29 Sep 2026).

## Before you start
- [ ] Be 18 or older.
- [ ] Have (or create) a personal **Adobe ID** (Enterprise/Federated IDs are not supported for Contributor).
- [ ] Decide country of residence as **South Africa** so tax and payment options match your documents.
- Sources: [Account and submission guidelines](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/get-started/manage-your-account/account-submission-guidelines.html), [Contributor overview](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/get-started/become-contributor/contributor-overview.html).

## 1. Create the Contributor account
- [ ] Open the Contributor portal: https://contributor.stock.adobe.com/
- [ ] Sign in with your Adobe ID (or create one), then complete profile onboarding.
- [ ] Accept the Contributor agreement.
- [ ] Confirm name, email, and country of residence match your legal documents.
- Sources: [Contributor overview](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/get-started/become-contributor/contributor-overview.html), [Account and submission guidelines](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/get-started/manage-your-account/account-submission-guidelines.html), portal https://contributor.stock.adobe.com/.

## 2. Verify contact details
- [ ] Verify email and phone number in the Contributor account settings.
- Source: help nav item "Verify your email and phone number" under Manage your account on Adobe Stock Contributor help (linked from [Contributor overview](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/get-started/become-contributor/contributor-overview.html)).

## 3. Tax form (W-8BEN for a non-US individual)
- [ ] In the Contributor portal, complete and e-sign **Form W-8BEN** in English (South Africa has a US tax treaty; the form claims treaty benefits).
- [ ] Without a valid form, Adobe withholds **30%** backup withholding.
- [ ] Form expires on the last day of the third calendar year after you sign it; renew before expiry.
- [ ] For company setups later, use the correct W-8 entity form with a tax advisor.
- Source: [Tax basics for non-U.S. citizens](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/payments-earnings/payment-taxes/tax-faq-non-us-citizens-registered-aliens.html).

## 4. Payment method (important for South Africa)
- [ ] Link a payment provider before you expect payouts.
- [ ] Adobe lists **Payoneer as a required payment provider** for Contributors in **South Africa** (also Indonesia, South Korea, Turkey). PayPal is listed as available globally, but follow what the portal offers for ZA.
- [ ] Payout eligibility (general): US $25 minimum royalties, and 45 days since first sale (unless a temporary Firefly-bonus exception applies).
- Source: [Payment requirements for Contributors](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/payments-earnings/payment-taxes/payment-requirements.html).

## 5. First upload: AI disclosure
- [ ] Only upload content whose generator licence allows commercial stock submission (Firefly commercially released outputs: see `from-cto-new/firefly-stock-resale-2026-09-29.md`).
- [ ] Tick **"Created using generative AI tools"** on every AI file.
- [ ] If a fictional person/property appears, also tick **"People and Property are fictional"**; this project's prompts avoid people.
- [ ] Titles under 70 characters; 25–49 keywords; include keyword `generative AI`; no artist names, brands, or "in the style of".
- [ ] Prefer 4 MP+ / ~4000 px on the long side after upscale; no watermarks; curate distinct keepers (avoid near-duplicate prompt spam).
- Sources: [Generative AI content guidelines](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/submit-your-content/submit-generative-ai-content/generative-ai-content-guidelines.html), [Submit generative AI content](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/submit-your-content/submit-generative-ai-content/submit-generative-ai-content.html), [Firefly FAQ for Adobe Stock](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/submit-your-content/submit-generative-ai-content/firefly-faq.html), [Distinct generative AI submission best practices](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/submit-your-content/submit-generative-ai-content/distinct-generative-ai-submission-best-practices.html).

## Order that usually works
1. Adobe ID + Contributor signup  
2. Verify email/phone  
3. W-8BEN  
4. Payoneer (ZA)  
5. Generate pilot keepers → QA → disclose AI → upload
